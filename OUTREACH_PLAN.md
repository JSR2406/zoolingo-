## 3. Contact Discovery Methods (Do NOT fabricate emails)

Never guess or invent personal email addresses. Use these legitimate channels:

1. **University faculty directories** — `.edu` / `.ac.in` domain staff pages almost always list email.
2. **Lab "Join Us" / "People" pages** — often list internship inquiries email.
3. **LinkedIn** — search "Professor [Name] [University]" or "[Company] Research Lead"; send a connection note + InMail.
4. **Twitter/X** — many academics post DM-open contact info.
5. **Google Scholar** — find recent papers on animal bioacoustics / Indic NLP, then email corresponding author.
6. **Conference proceedings** — ICASSP, Interspeech, EMNLP, NeurIPS (ML for science) animal-communication workshops.
