## PERSONALIZATION CHEAT SHEET

| Recipient Type | What to Reference | Where to Find It |
|----------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Professor / Lab Lead | Their recent paper, lab name, specific research area (bioacoustics, NLP, speech) | Google Scholar, lab website, faculty page |
| Company (SARVAM AI, Murf, etc.) | Their specific product you use (Bulbul v3, Sarvam-105B, Falcon TTS) | Their website, product page, press release |
| Pet-tech startup | Their product, market focus, recent funding/launch | LinkedIn, Wellfound, TechCrunch/YourStory |

**Golden rules:**
1. Never send a template with brackets still in it.
2. Keep the first email under ~150 words.
3. Include exactly TWO links: live demo + GitHub.
4. Mention "paid" and "open-source" early — those are the hooks.
5. Follow up exactly twice, then move on.
